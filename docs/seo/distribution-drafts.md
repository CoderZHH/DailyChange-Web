## 中文

标题：〖开发者自荐〗DailyChange：每天留一张自拍，回看自己慢慢变化

我是 DailyChange 的开发者，想介绍一个 iPhone 上的每日自拍记录 App。

每天拍一张自己的照片，App 会在设备上检测并对齐双眼。过一段时间，可以选一段日子，把照片连成变化视频，保存到相册。以前拍过的自拍也能导入并确认日期。

我希望它除了拍照，也能让人愿意回来翻一翻：照片会留在相纸和日历中；想多记一点，可以选择 PRO 的留言、日记、贴纸、照片墙和未来信件。

每日拍照、导入、历史浏览与视频导出属于基础功能。PRO 有年费与终身购买选项，价格以商店为准。对齐在 iPhone 上完成，可选备份存入你自己的 iCloud；恢复购买只恢复购买权益，不会找回照片。

它目前围绕同一个人的单人正脸自拍设计。漏拍的日子留白，不会生成一张补上。欢迎反馈：持续记录时，你最容易卡在拍摄、对齐还是回看？

## English

Title: I made DailyChange, an iPhone selfie journal with eye alignment and timelapse export

I’m the developer of DailyChange. It lets you keep one selfie a day, align your eyes on your iPhone, and turn a date range into a timelapse saved to Photos. You can import older selfies and confirm their dates, too.

The app keeps your photos in a calendar and paper-style prints. Optional PRO features add notes, diary entries, stickers, photo walls, and letters to your future self.

Daily photos, importing, browsing, and video export are Basic features. PRO offers an annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Photos and alignment data stay on your device; optional backups go to your own iCloud. Restore Purchases restores purchase access, not missing photos.

It is designed for clear, front-facing photos of the same person. Missed days stay blank. I’d like feedback on what makes a daily selfie routine difficult to keep up: taking the photo, getting consistent framing, or having a reason to look back.
